# Attribution
This supervisor playbook is an original adaptation of planning, independent-domain delegation, fresh-context task execution, review and durable-ledger ideas from obra/superpowers by Jesse Vincent. It changes upstream policies: bounded parallel worktrees instead of unconditional serial implementation; no acceptance waivers at retry caps; enforced root-shared caps and external-action approval boundaries. No upstream scripts are bundled or required.
